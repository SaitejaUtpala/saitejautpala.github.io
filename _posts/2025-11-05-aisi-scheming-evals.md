I designed and implemented **dangerous-capability evaluations** of LLM agents, including
**AI scheming evals**. This was part of the UK AISI
[evals bounty](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/evals-bounty) for novel evaluations of frontier systems.

Dangerous-capability evals test whether agents can carry out, or take precursor steps toward,
harmful autonomous actions. Scheming evals ask a narrower question: whether an agent will
**hide its goals, sandbag under evaluation, or work around oversight** when that helps it
complete a task.

I won **£20,025** for this work.
