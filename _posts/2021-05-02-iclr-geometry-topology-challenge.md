Won **2nd place ($750)** at the
[ICLR 2021 Computational Geometry & Topology Challenge](https://gt-rl.github.io/2021/challenge).

The submission was *Shrinkage Estimator of SPD matrices & Recursive Karcher Expectation Estimator*,
built with [Geomstats](https://github.com/geomstats/geomstats). 2nd and 3rd place were co-awarded at $750 each.
